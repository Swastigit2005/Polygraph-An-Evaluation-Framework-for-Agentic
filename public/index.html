        Same documents, same answer-writing model. Plain AI search is the standard recipe: search once,
        then answer from whatever comes back. Polygraph adds a sharper search and the fact-check-and-retry loop.
      
    
      
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    How it works
    
      
    Three ideas, one honest assistant.
    
      
    
        
    
          1
          
    Search two ways
    
          
    Keyword search catches exact terms like daemon.json; meaning-based search catches paraphrases.
            Results are merged and re-ranked by a third model.
    
        
    
        
    
          2
          
    A second AI checks the evidence
    
          
    Before anything is written, a fast "grader" model decides whether the snippets actually
            contain the answer, and says why.
    
        
    
        
    
          3
          
    Retry, then refuse
    
          
    Weak evidence? The agent rewrites its search (up to twice). Still nothing? It says so,
            instead of inventing an answer.
